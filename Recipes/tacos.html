Taco Recipe

Mattea's Favorite Tacos

A taco is a traditional Mexican dish made of a folded or rolled corn or flour tortilla filled with a savory ingredient, such as seasoned meat, beans, or vegetables.

Needed Ingredients

  • Meat of Your Choosing
  • Cotija Cheese
  • Seasoned Cabbage
  • Fried Corn Tortillas
  • Parmesan Crust on Tortillas
  1. 1. Brown the beef: Cook the beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes until fully cooked, breaking it apart with a spoon. Add your spices and stir well.
  2. 2. Simmer: Reduce heat to medium, add tomato sauce and water, and cook for another 7–8 minutes until slightly thickened.
  3. 3. Warm shells: Warm your tortillas or hard shells according to package instructions.
  4. 4. Assemble: Spoon the meat mixture into the shells and top with your favorite fresh vegetables, cheese, and salsa.
