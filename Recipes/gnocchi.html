Gnocchi Recipe

Mattea's Favorite Gnocchi

Gnocchi are soft, pillowy Italian dumplings made from mashed potatoes, flour, and sometimes eggs.

Needed Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Flour
  • Eggs and Egg Yolks
  • Salt
  • Sauce of your Choosing
  1. 1. Prep the Gnocchi
  2. 2. Shape the Gnocchi with a ricer tool to mash the potatoes
  3. 3. Mix gently
  4. 4. Prep the water to boil
  5. 5. Gnocchi cooks super fast, then top with your sauce!
